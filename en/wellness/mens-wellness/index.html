Ingredients for this topic

Minerals

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral that the body cannot produce on its own. It plays a role in enzyme activity, protein synthesis, cell division and the normal function of the immune system, making it one of the minerals the body needs to obtain in adequate amounts from food.

Minerals

Selenium

Selenium plays a role in the activity of enzymes involved in protecting cells from oxidative stress, as well as in the normal function of the immune system and the thyroid gland. It can be obtained from a wide variety of foods.

Botanical Extracts

Siberian Ginseng

Siberian ginseng is a herbal plant that is distinct from common ginseng, with active compounds in the eleutheroside group found in its roots and rhizomes. It is one of the plants that has attracted interest in studies on fatigue, physical endurance and the stress response.

Vitamins

Vitamin C

Vitamin C is a water-soluble vitamin and an essential nutrient that the human body cannot produce on its own. It plays a role in collagen formation, the normal function of the immune system, and increasing the absorption of iron from plant-based foods. It is found in a wide variety of vegetables and fruits.

Botanical Extracts

Reishi Mushroom

Reishi mushroom is a mushroom of the genus Ganoderma that has long been revered in Chinese culture, earning it the nickname “the mushroom of immortality”. It is currently attracting interest in studies of its bioactive compounds, particularly beta-glucans and triterpenes.

Vitamins

Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 plays an important role in nervous system function, red blood cell formation and the immune system. It is also involved in the metabolism of homocysteine, an amino acid that has attracted interest in studies of vascular health.

Amino Acids

Taurine

Taurine is a sulfur-containing compound found naturally in the body, particularly in the heart, brain, muscles and retina. It plays a role in maintaining cellular balance, nervous system function and the formation of bile compounds.

Amino Acids

L-Arginine

L-Arginine is an amino acid that plays a role in protein synthesis and in the production of nitric oxide, a signalling molecule involved in blood vessel function. It also takes part in the removal of ammonia and the synthesis of important compounds in the body.

Fungal Extracts

Cordyceps

Cordyceps is a name used for several species of fungi with a history of use in Asian herbal formulas, especially Tibetan cordyceps and golden cordyceps. They contain active compounds of interest such as cordycepin, adenosine and polysaccharides, and are currently being studied in relation to cellular energy production and physical performance.

Botanical Extracts

Korean Ginseng

Korean ginseng is an herb with a long history of use in Asia, particularly its root, which contains key compounds known as ginsenosides. It is currently attracting interest in relation to energy and fatigue, immune system function and men's health.

Heart & CirculationMen's Wellness

Vitamins

Niacin

Niacin, or vitamin B3, plays an important role in the body's energy-yielding processes as a component in the formation of substances called NAD and NADP, which help cells use nutrients from carbohydrates, proteins and fats in various processes. Niacin also contributes to the maintenance of normal mucous membranes of the digestive tract, normal skin and normal nervous system function.

Marine-Derived Ingredients

Oyster

Oysters are a seafood rich in a variety of nutrients, especially zinc, vitamin B12, iron and other minerals, which play an important part in immune function, protein building, cellular processes and men's overall health.

Botanical Extracts

Ginkgo Biloba

Ginkgo biloba leaf is part of a plant with a long history of use. Ginkgo leaf extract contains key compounds, namely flavonoids and terpene lactones, which are attracting interest in studies on memory, brain function and blood circulation.
