Minerals

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral that the body cannot produce on its own. It plays a role in enzyme activity, protein synthesis, cell division and the normal function of the immune system, making it one of the minerals the body needs to obtain in adequate amounts from food.

Botanical Extracts

Astragalus

Astragalus (known in Thai as Pak Kee or Aung Kee) is a herbal plant of the legume family whose root has a long history of use in traditional Chinese medicine. The root contains several groups of natural compounds, including polysaccharides, astragalosides and flavonoids, which have been studied for their relationship with cellular function, the immune system and antioxidant processes.

Botanical Extracts

Reishi Mushroom

Reishi mushroom is a mushroom of the genus Ganoderma that has long been revered in Chinese culture, earning it the nickname “the mushroom of immortality”. It is currently attracting interest in studies of its bioactive compounds, particularly beta-glucans and triterpenes.

Amino Acids

L-Cysteine

L-Cysteine is an amino acid that plays an important role in protein synthesis, especially of keratin, a component of hair and nails. It is also one of the precursors the body uses to produce glutathione, a substance involved in the cell's antioxidant system.

Amino Acids

Taurine

Taurine is a sulfur-containing compound found naturally in the body, particularly in the heart, brain, muscles and retina. It plays a role in maintaining cellular balance, nervous system function and the formation of bile compounds.

Botanical Extracts

Ginger

Ginger is an herbal plant that has long been used both in food and in traditional knowledge. It contains key compounds known as gingerols and shogaols, which contribute to its distinctive hot, spicy taste. Ginger is currently attracting research interest in relation to the digestive system and gastric motility.

Botanical Extracts

Black Pepper

Black pepper contains piperine, the key component responsible for its characteristic pungent heat. Beyond its culinary use, piperine has also attracted interest in studies on the absorption and metabolism of certain substances in the body.

Botanical Extracts

Bilberry

Bilberry is a fruit of the berry family containing active compounds in the anthocyanin group, which has led researchers to take an interest in bilberry in relation to eye health, blood vessel function and the response to oxidative stress.

Botanical Extracts

Turmeric

Turmeric is a herbal plant with a yellow-orange rhizome that is widely used as a spice in Asian cuisine. It contains active compounds in the curcuminoid group, especially curcumin, which has attracted researchers' interest in relation to inflammatory processes, oxidative stress and the digestive system.

Botanical Extracts

Green Tea

Green tea is a beverage familiar to many people. It contains key compounds such as catechins and EGCG, which are natural antioxidants. These compounds are currently attracting researchers' interest in relation to energy metabolism, blood vessel function and the protection of cells from damage caused by free radicals.
