Botanical Extracts

Astragalus

Astragalus (known in Thai as Pak Kee or Aung Kee) is a herbal plant of the legume family whose root has a long history of use in traditional Chinese medicine. The root contains several groups of natural compounds, including polysaccharides, astragalosides and flavonoids, which have been studied for their relationship with cellular function, the immune system and antioxidant processes.

Minerals

Selenium

Selenium plays a role in the activity of enzymes involved in protecting cells from oxidative stress, as well as in the normal function of the immune system and the thyroid gland. It can be obtained from a wide variety of foods.

Vitamins

Vitamin C

Vitamin C is a water-soluble vitamin and an essential nutrient that the human body cannot produce on its own. It plays a role in collagen formation, the normal function of the immune system, and increasing the absorption of iron from plant-based foods. It is found in a wide variety of vegetables and fruits.

Botanical Extracts

Reishi Mushroom

Reishi mushroom is a mushroom of the genus Ganoderma that has long been revered in Chinese culture, earning it the nickname “the mushroom of immortality”. It is currently attracting interest in studies of its bioactive compounds, particularly beta-glucans and triterpenes.

Vitamins

Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 plays an important role in nervous system function, red blood cell formation and the immune system. It is also involved in the metabolism of homocysteine, an amino acid that has attracted interest in studies of vascular health.

Fungal Extracts

Cordyceps

Cordyceps is a name used for several species of fungi with a history of use in Asian herbal formulas, especially Tibetan cordyceps and golden cordyceps. They contain active compounds of interest such as cordycepin, adenosine and polysaccharides, and are currently being studied in relation to cellular energy production and physical performance.

Botanical Extracts

Korean Ginseng

Korean ginseng is an herb with a long history of use in Asia, particularly its root, which contains key compounds known as ginsenosides. It is currently attracting interest in relation to energy and fatigue, immune system function and men's health.

Vitamins

Vitamin E

Vitamin E is a fat-soluble vitamin that plays an important role in protecting cells from oxidative stress and is also involved in the normal function of the immune system. Vitamin E is found in vegetable oils, nuts, seeds and certain vegetables.

Marine-Derived Ingredients

Oyster

Oysters are a seafood rich in a variety of nutrients, especially zinc, vitamin B12, iron and other minerals, which play an important part in immune function, protein building, cellular processes and men's overall health.

Antioxidants

Pine Bark Extract

Pine bark extract is obtained from the bark of certain pine species and is rich in natural polyphenols, especially procyanidins, which have antioxidant properties related to skin health, cellular function and the vascular system.

Vitamins

Vitamin D3

Vitamin D3 plays an important role in the absorption of calcium and phosphorus to maintain normal bones and teeth. It is also involved in muscle and immune system function, and the body can produce it when the skin receives enough UVB radiation from sunlight.
