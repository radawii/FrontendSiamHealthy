Minerals

Selenium

Selenium plays a role in the activity of enzymes involved in protecting cells from oxidative stress, as well as in the normal function of the immune system and the thyroid gland. It can be obtained from a wide variety of foods.

Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 plays an important role in nervous system function, red blood cell formation and the immune system. It is also involved in the metabolism of homocysteine, an amino acid that has attracted interest in studies of vascular health.

Bilberry

Bilberry is a fruit of the berry family containing active compounds in the anthocyanin group, which has led researchers to take an interest in bilberry in relation to eye health, blood vessel function and the response to oxidative stress.

Vitamins

Vitamin D3

Vitamin D3 plays an important role in the absorption of calcium and phosphorus to maintain normal bones and teeth. It is also involved in muscle and immune system function, and the body can produce it when the skin receives enough UVB radiation from sunlight.

Vitamins

Vitamin B12

Vitamin B12 is a water-soluble vitamin that plays an important role in nervous system function, red blood cell formation and DNA synthesis. It is found naturally in animal foods such as meat, fish, eggs and dairy products. Although the body needs only a small amount, an adequate intake is necessary for its various processes to function normally.

Botanical Extracts

Astragalus

Astragalus (known in Thai as Pak Kee or Aung Kee) is a herbal plant of the legume family whose root has a long history of use in traditional Chinese medicine. The root contains several groups of natural compounds, including polysaccharides, astragalosides and flavonoids, which have been studied for their relationship with cellular function, the immune system and antioxidant processes.

Siberian Ginseng

Siberian ginseng is a herbal plant that is distinct from common ginseng, with active compounds in the eleutheroside group found in its roots and rhizomes. It is one of the plants that has attracted interest in studies on fatigue, physical endurance and the stress response.

Botanical Extracts

Reishi Mushroom

Reishi mushroom is a mushroom of the genus Ganoderma that has long been revered in Chinese culture, earning it the nickname “the mushroom of immortality”. It is currently attracting interest in studies of its bioactive compounds, particularly beta-glucans and triterpenes.

Amino Acids

Taurine

Taurine is a sulfur-containing compound found naturally in the body, particularly in the heart, brain, muscles and retina. It plays a role in maintaining cellular balance, nervous system function and the formation of bile compounds.

Botanical Extracts

Ginger

Ginger is an herbal plant that has long been used both in food and in traditional knowledge. It contains key compounds known as gingerols and shogaols, which contribute to its distinctive hot, spicy taste. Ginger is currently attracting research interest in relation to the digestive system and gastric motility.

Black Pepper

Black pepper contains piperine, the key component responsible for its characteristic pungent heat. Beyond its culinary use, piperine has also attracted interest in studies on the absorption and metabolism of certain substances in the body.

Botanical Extracts

Turmeric

Turmeric is a herbal plant with a yellow-orange rhizome that is widely used as a spice in Asian cuisine. It contains active compounds in the curcuminoid group, especially curcumin, which has attracted researchers' interest in relation to inflammatory processes, oxidative stress and the digestive system.

Botanical Extracts

Green Tea

Green tea is a beverage familiar to many people. It contains key compounds such as catechins and EGCG, which are natural antioxidants. These compounds are currently attracting researchers' interest in relation to energy metabolism, blood vessel function and the protection of cells from damage caused by free radicals.

Minerals

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral that the body cannot produce on its own. It plays a role in enzyme activity, protein synthesis, cell division and the normal function of the immune system, making it one of the minerals the body needs to obtain in adequate amounts from food.

Vitamins

Vitamin C

Vitamin C is a water-soluble vitamin and an essential nutrient that the human body cannot produce on its own. It plays a role in collagen formation, the normal function of the immune system, and increasing the absorption of iron from plant-based foods. It is found in a wide variety of vegetables and fruits.

Botanical Extracts

Grape Seed Extract

Grape seed extract is a source of key compounds such as proanthocyanidins, or OPCs, which have antioxidant properties and are attracting research interest in relation to blood vessel health, cell function and the skin.

Amino Acids

L-Cysteine

L-Cysteine is an amino acid that plays an important role in protein synthesis, especially of keratin, a component of hair and nails. It is also one of the precursors the body uses to produce glutathione, a substance involved in the cell's antioxidant system.

Bones & JointsWeight & Metabolism
Learn more
Vitamins

Vitamin E

Vitamin E is a fat-soluble vitamin that plays an important role in protecting cells from oxidative stress and is also involved in the normal function of the immune system. Vitamin E is found in vegetable oils, nuts, seeds and certain vegetables.

Heart & CirculationBones & Joints
Learn more
Vitamins

Niacin

Niacin, or vitamin B3, plays an important role in the body's energy-yielding processes as a component in the formation of substances called NAD and NADP, which help cells use nutrients from carbohydrates, proteins and fats in various processes. Niacin also contributes to the maintenance of normal mucous membranes of the digestive tract, normal skin and normal nervous system function.

Antioxidants

Pine Bark Extract

Pine bark extract is obtained from the bark of certain pine species and is rich in natural polyphenols, especially procyanidins, which have antioxidant properties related to skin health, cellular function and the vascular system.
